# Business Rules

## Operating model

Perkasa Motors is an operating vehicle-trading business. Its primary model is sourcing, acquiring, preparing, and selling vehicle units. Financial reporting must support these operations and preserve auditable unit economics.

Partner funding is collaboration and operating support for unit activity. It is not an investor ROI product. Investor-style analytics must not be reintroduced as the main operating model.

## Unit funding

The new architecture supports two unit funding sources:

- Perkasa Funding: capital deployed by Perkasa for a unit.
- Partner Funding: capital supplied by a partner for a unit under a separate agreement.

Partner Funding is not Founder Capital and does not automatically create company equity. Funding percentage, Profit Share percentage, and Loss Exposure percentage are independent concepts. A partner's Profit Share must come from the applicable agreement or policy, not from its Funding percentage. By default, partner Loss Exposure is pro rata to funding unless the agreement explicitly sets another valid allocation.

## Unit economics

Base Unit Cost contains only direct costs attributable to the unit, including purchase, service, repair, spare parts, transport, document or tax costs, direct marketing, and other genuinely direct unit costs.

Payroll, office costs, software, and general overhead are business operating expenses. They are not Unit Cost.

Unit Cost and Unit Funding are separate. A Funding Gap does not by itself prove an unrecorded cash payment. Purchase cost and funding can describe one acquisition event, so cash reporting records the underlying cash movement only once.

On sale, Sale Proceeds, Capital Return, partner settlement, and Realized Profit are classifications of one sale event. They must not be recorded as separate duplicate cash inflows.

## Profit, loss, and reserve

Funding, Profit Share, and Loss Exposure must remain independently calculated and displayed.

For new units, the reserve policy is 10% of positive realized Perkasa profit unless a valid unit-specific override exists. No reserve is created from zero or negative Perkasa profit. Reserve is a restricted classification within cash unless an actual transfer occurred.

## Legacy boundary

Legacy Accounting remains legacy. Preserve recorded legacy allocations, returns, partner terms, unit economics, and classifications as historical facts. Do not retroactively rewrite them to conform to the new architecture. Route new units and new transactions through the new operating model.

Legacy units with accounting dependencies must be archived when removed from active use. They must not be hard-deleted.
