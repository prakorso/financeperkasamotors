Operational collaboration

Partner Funding

Company-level relationship, funding exposure, and settlement context. Vehicle-specific funding remains visible in Inventory.

Cost ≠ funding. Partner funding identifies who financed economic cost; it is not an additional unit expense. Funding Exposure %, Partner Profit %, and Loss Exposure % are independent.
